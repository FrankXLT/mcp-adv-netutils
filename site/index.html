What You Can Do

Everything needed to find and fix outages fast

Built for everyday operators, home labs, multi-VLAN networks, and AI assistants connected over MCP.

Pinpoint Where Connections Break

Test local router, VPN tunnel, ISP path, packet-size ceiling, open ports, TLS certificates, and split-horizon DNS side by side so you know whether the fault is local, upstream, or in DNS.

Saved Destinations & Scan Pinned

Bookmark your core gateway, NAS, websites, and VPN peers with a default check and optional Wake MAC, pin favorites to the top bar, sweep all pinned targets with Scan Pinned, or toggle 5-minute monitoring.

Network Studio (/web) & ECharts

Explore interactive Apache ECharts path whiskers, HTTP/TLS timing waterfalls, DNS comparisons, and 24h/7d/30d uptime timelines across 4 themes, switch between Chart and Table views, and copy summaries or TSV data.

Unattended 5-Minute Monitoring

Schedule automatic background checks that ignore single-packet glitches and alert only after 2 consecutive failed checks. Open any past run over the last 30 days and click Run Again & Compare.

Inspect & Wake LAN Hardware

Scan your local network to group real hardware (with manufacturer names) separately from containers and VMs, compare against a saved baseline, check switch ports, and wake sleeping servers with Wake-on-LAN.

33 MCP Tools Over Two Doorways

Expose all 33 tools cleanly to AI assistants locally via --stdio, on your private network at /lan/{LAN_MCP_TOKEN}/mcp (and /sse), or remotely at /{PATH_SECRET}/mcp behind a 30-minute operator passphrase lock.

Plain-Language Guide

How remote access, safety locks, and history stay simple

Try every panel safely in the Live Demo or inspect tool parameters and deployment guides in the Docs.

Separate Remote & LAN Doorways

Remote MCP stays isolated from local-only endpoints

Remote traffic enters through /{PATH_SECRET}/mcp and /web with passphrase protection, while local /lan/{LAN_MCP_TOKEN}/mcp and /sse only accept private LAN peers and reject proxy forwarding headers.

30-Minute Operator Passphrase

Sensitive checks stay locked until you unlock them

Port scans, LAN discovery, Saved Destination edits, and Wake-on-LAN stay locked until you enter your operator passphrase—then automatically re-lock after 30 minutes or whenever you click lock.

Real Hardware vs. Containers & VMs

Spot physical servers separately from container churn

Local Network discovery separates physical appliances and vendor names from temporary container or VM addresses, and lets you save a baseline to highlight added, removed, or changed devices.

2-Failure Alert Rule & 30-Day History

No noisy false alarms, plus side-by-side comparisons

Unattended 5-minute checks only raise an alert after 2 consecutive failed checks, while keeping 30 days of past runs ready to open, copy, or re-test with Run Again & Compare.
